# 0001. Record architecture decisions

Date: {{YYYY-MM-DD}}

## Status

Accepted

## Context

We need to record the architectural decisions made on this project, along with the
reasoning behind them. Without a record, the reasoning is lost with the people who
held it, and later contributors either re-litigate settled questions or unknowingly
undo a decision that was load-bearing.

## Decision

We will use Architecture Decision Records, as described by Michael Nygard.

Records live in `docs/adr/`, numbered sequentially and zero-padded to four digits
(`0001-`, `0002-`, …). Each record has the sections: Status, Context, Decision,
Consequences.

Records are immutable. A decision that is later reversed is not edited — a new
record supersedes it, and the old record's Status is updated to point at the new one.

## Consequences

Decisions become searchable and attributable, and new contributors can read why the
system is shaped the way it is rather than inferring it.

The cost is discipline: a decision not written down is invisible, so the practice
only pays off if records are actually created at the time. Records also add friction
to reversing a decision — deliberately, but it is friction nonetheless.
